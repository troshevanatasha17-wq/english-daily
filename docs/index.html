Времена

6 тем · 1–6
1–6

1Past Simple

Основные события в прошлом, которые идут одно за другим (в рассказе — главная линия истории). Форма: утверждение — subject + V2 (past verb), отрицание — didn't + базовая форма, вопрос — Did + subject + базовая форма. Для to be — was / were. Отличие от used to: Past Simple — сделал один раз.

Примеры

  • I traveled through Italy with a group from university. My friends persuaded me to go.
  • I went to circus — I did it once.
  • Yesterday she bought a new jacket.
  • She pulled out a carrot from the ground. She realised it was the ring…

2Past Continuous

Форма: was / were + V-ing (I was working / I wasn't working / Was I working?). Используется:

  • для действия в процессе в конкретный момент прошлого;
  • для фона в истории;
  • для двух действий, которые шли одновременно;
  • для действия, которое прервали другим действием (Past Continuous + Past Simple).

Примеры

  • I was watching TV at 8 p.m.
  • It was raining while we were walking. She was reading while he was cooking.
  • I was sleeping when the phone rang.
  • I was walking along the beach when I realised that…
  • I was riding the subway. I was halfway to work when…
  • While I was listening to…, the audience was singing along. The people around were dancing.
  • While we were watching the film, we heard a song that reminded me of my childhood.
  • They were performing live at the festival when the lights went out.

3Past Perfect

Форма: had + V3. Действие, которое произошло раньше другого действия в прошлом (Grammar bank 1A narrative tenses).

Примеры

  • When I got to the top floor, I realised I had left my phone in the car.
  • When we arrived at the station, the train had already left.
  • I fainted because I had drunk too much.
  • She realised it was the ring she had lost last year.
  • The band had already played their biggest hit when we arrived.
  • I have never seen a cockroach. Before that I had never seen a cockroach in a flat.

4Present Perfect

Форма: have / has + V3 (I have seen that movie / I haven't seen that movie / Have you seen that movie?). Используется:

  1. для действий в неуказанное время в прошлом (точное время не называется);
  2. для действий, которые начались в прошлом и продолжаются сейчас;
  3. с выражениями времени ever, never, already, yet, just, since, for, recently;
  4. для действий, у которых есть результат в настоящем;
  5. для жизненного опыта и достижений.

Примеры

  • I have visited Paris. She has lived in this city for five years.
  • They have never been to Australia. I have just finished my work.
  • He has broken his arm, so he can't play football today.
  • I have read that book before. I have never been to a concert like that before.
  • Yulia hasn't given birth yet. I haven't been to the gym yet. We haven't bought a present for Yulia yet.
  • Have you done your homework yet? Have you called your mum yet? Have you had a class with Katya yet?
  • Have you ever been to Fethiye?
  • I haven't listened to their songs for a long time.

5Present Perfect Continuous

Форма: have / has been + V-ing. На доске — для действия, которое длится до сих пор, вместе с for + период.

Примеры

  • I've been listening to them for 5 years.
  • They have been running this hotel for 30 years.
  • We've been working together for a year.

6Future in the past: past plans and intentions

Когда мы рассказываем о планах и ожиданиях в прошлом, будущее «сдвигается» назад: I think I will cry (Present + Future) → I thought I would cry (Past Simple + Future in the past). Справочник 4A:

  • was / were going to — запланированное действие, которое не произошло. Отрицание — wasn't / weren't going to.
  • planned to, hoped to — похожее значение, в Past Simple или Past Continuous. Отрицание — wasn't planning to.
  • was expecting to, was waiting to — другие глаголы в Past Continuous для прошлых намерений.
  • meant to — план, который не случился, чаще в Past Simple.
  • didn't mean to — когда кто-то сделал что-то случайно.
  • intended to — более формально, чем meant to или hoped to; используется и чтобы назвать причину действия. Отрицание — didn't intend.
  • thought it would be — причина запланированного действия в прошлом.

Примеры

  • I was going to call you, but I was too busy. They were going to visit the museum, but it was closed.
  • I planned to visit last week, but I couldn't in the end. I was hoping to meet your father, but he wasn't there.
  • I wasn't planning to go anywhere this summer.
  • I was expecting to go to Turkey that year.
  • I meant to introduce you to Xavier, but he left early. Sorry — I crashed the car! I didn't mean to!
  • The doctor intended to see me last week, but he had to cancel. He bought the ring because he intended to ask her to marry him.
  • I brought you because I thought it would be nice if you met my parents.
  • They planned to become rappers. They hadn't intended to lie for so long. They weren't expecting to become global superstars.

7Used to и would: привычки в прошлом

Used to — привычки и состояния в прошлом, которых больше нет (in the past, no longer true). Форма: I used to smoke / Did you use to smoke? / I didn't use to smoke. Отличие от Past Simple: Past Simple — сделал один раз, used to — делал много раз.

Would — тоже для повторяющихся действий в прошлом. Нельзя использовать would со stative verbs (be, have, know, like, love, hate, think) и с вредными привычками, которые бросили: там только used to.

Примеры

  • I went to circus (did it once). I used to go to circus (did it many times).
  • I was fat → I used to be fat. I worked in this company → I used to work in this company.
  • I smoked (not clear) → I used to smoke (broke this bad habit).
  • I used to stay up late when I was at school. I would / I'd go to bed at 2 or 3 am.
  • When I was a child, we used to spend every summer at my grandparents' house.
  • It's not as common as it used to be. There used to be fewer places.

8Be used to и get used to

Be used to — состояние: уже привык, это нормально.

Get used to — процесс: привыкать (недавно привык или ещё привыкаю).

После обеих конструкций идёт существительное или gerund (verb + ing).

Примеры

  • I'm used to it — normal, I've lived here for 10 years.
  • People from Paris are used to the beauty of the city.
  • We are not used to the climate, the food.
  • I'm getting used to waking up early. I got used to it — recently. You'll get used to it!
  • I'd never get used to working at nights.
  • As soon as you get used to your anxiety level, you have to get used to even bigger projects.
  • It was hard to get used to waking up early. I haven't got used to sitting at home and not working yet.

9Present и Past Continuous для раздражающих привычек

Справочник 2C That's annoying!: Present и Past Continuous с always, constantly, continually, forever используют для привычки, которая нас раздражает. Иногда эта же структура показывает положительную эмоцию. Фразы раздражения (I can't stand it, It really annoys me, It gets on my nerves, It drives me crazy) можно продолжить when + clause, а также relative clause или -ing.

Примеры

  • You're always losing your phone! My dad's forever complaining about noise.
  • Our car was continually breaking down.
  • Children at this age are so cute. They're constantly laughing!
  • In my block the guy next door is continually singing along with his favourite songs.
  • This light above my desk drives me mad. It's constantly buzzing.
  • It drives me crazy when… It gets on my nerves when people use their phone right next to me.
  • Children that misbehave in restaurants really bug me. I can't stand people shortening my name.
  • It drove me crazy. I couldn't stand it. It kept happening.
  • It doesn't bother me at all. I don't mind it.

10Passive voice

Форма пассива: be + V3. Меняется только be, в зависимости от времени. Справочник 7B:

  • пассив используют, чтобы сфокусироваться на человеке или предмете, на которого направлено действие, а не на том, кто его совершает;
  • когда исполнитель очевиден, неизвестен или не важен;
  • если важно, кто совершил действие, его называют с by;
  • в более формальных ситуациях: в научных и академических текстах, в новостях;
  • с just, yet, still, already в Present Perfect passive (обратите внимание на место наречия);
  • get + V3 — неформально, когда случилось что-то драматичное или неожиданное, часто негативное.
Время Форма Пример с доски
Present Simple am / is / are + V3 Dopamine is released into our brains.
Present Continuous am / is / are + being + V3 We are being followed. More series are being made.
Past Simple was / were + V3 TV viewing was controlled by TV channels.
Present Perfect have / has been + V3 Binge watching has been criticised by doctors.
Past Perfect had been + V3 All better rooms had already been booked.
Future Simple will be + V3 will be chosen
Be going to am / is / are going to be + V3 am going to be chosen
Модальные глаголы can / should / must / might + be + V3 It should be done. It must be done.

Другие примеры

  • The app has been downloaded over a million times.
  • Jude was arrested at the festival last week. The museum was built in the 1980s. The winner will be announced tomorrow.
  • The play was written by Shakespeare in 1609.
  • My train has just been cancelled. The report has already been signed, but it hasn't been sent yet. I still haven't been paid for last month's work.
  • I got stopped by the police last night! One of my lights wasn't working.
  • The show was criticized for being too rushed and confusing. It shouldn't have been canceled.
  • The finale was spoiled on social media. Fans were disappointed by the last season.
  • I was told to be polite and never ruin surprises for others.
  • Isabella's photo has already been posted 5,000 times online.
  • I've just been tagged in a photo. My phone got damaged recently. I'm being sent a lot of spam at the moment.
  • She found out that her photo had been used.

Условные предложения

4 темы · 11–14
11–14

11Zero и First Conditional

Zero Conditional — то, что всегда правда: условие → результат.

First Conditional — реальное условие и возможный результат в будущем: if + Present Simple, will + глагол.

Вместо if может быть when (когда это точно случится). What if + Present Simple — «а что если…?».

Примеры

  • If it's 0 degrees, water freezes.
  • If I don't sleep well, I usually feel terrible.
  • If I have time, I'll call you. If I don't have money, I'll borrow it from you.
  • If I have one English lesson instead of two, it will affect my level.
  • When I get home, I'll call you. When I become a millionaire, I won't call you.
  • What if you don't like it?

12Unless, even if, in case

Unless + Present Simple = if not: условие, без которого что-то не произойдёт.

Even if — даже если.

In case + clause — на случай, если.

In case of + noun — в случае чего-то.

Примеры

  • I'm not gonna stop learning English unless they fire me.
  • Unless it rains, we'll go for a walk. Unless we leave now, we'll miss the bus.
  • Unless you save money, you won't be able to travel.
  • You won't learn anything unless you pay attention in class.
  • Unless you invite her, Akira won't come.
  • Even if you study 7 languages, it won't affect your English level.
  • Alex will help me even if he doesn't want to. I'll travel alone even if I feel lonelier that way.
  • Take a jumper with you in case it gets cold. You should leave now in case you miss the bus.
  • I'll definitely study the local language first in case no one speaks English.
  • Call this number in case of emergency. I'm going to take a first aid kit in case of accidents.

13Second Conditional

Гипотетическое настоящее и будущее: if + Past Simple, would + infinitive (в результате могут быть также could, might). Разбирали на моральных дилеммах (скачивание книг без оплаты) и гипотетических вопросах.

Примеры

  • If I were a dragon, I would burn some villages.
  • If I were in a bookshop, I wouldn't steal a book. If I saw a book, I would steal it.
  • I'd try to download it for free if I really needed to read it.
  • If it was in the library, you could get it for free anyway.
  • If it was available and I really needed to read it, I might download it as an e-book.
  • If you could be any fictional character, who would you be and why?
  • What would you do if you could solve any unsolved mystery?
  • It would be nice if… Wouldn't it be scary if…?

14Third и Mixed Conditional

Third Conditional — гипотетическое прошлое, то, чего не случилось: if + Past Perfect (had + V3), would have + V3.

Mixed Conditional — условие в прошлом, результат в настоящем: if + Past Perfect, would + глагол.

Примеры

  • If I had money, I'd buy a new car — present / future. If I had had money, I would've bought a car — in the past.
  • If I had heard about it 5 years ago, I wouldn't have believed it.
  • If I had prepared, I would have answered better.
  • If the atmosphere had been more fun, you would have liked it more.
  • If I had watched a ton of documentaries, I would be very smart (mixed). I would've become… (third).

15Модальные глаголы обязанности и разрешения

Разбирали на темах правил в офисе, дресс-кода и вакансий.

  • Must — сильная обязанность, часто внутренняя (от самого говорящего), используется как эмоциональная рекомендация. В разговорной речи must встречается редко.
  • Have to — обязанность извне: от начальника, правил, расписания. Разговорный вариант — I've got to = gotta go.
  • Be supposed to — ожидание, неформальное требование, которое не всегда строго соблюдается.
  • Should — совет, рекомендация.
  • Can't = mustn't = be not allowed — запрет.
  • Don't have to — нет необходимости.

Примеры

  • I must be on time for work. I have to be on time for work. I'm supposed to be on time for work.
  • You must not enter the lab without safety goggles.
  • You are not permitted to use a smartphone, though you are allowed to use a dictionary.
  • It's necessary to register if you want to borrow a book. It's essential to use a seatbelt at all times.
  • You've got to show your passport or a national identity card.
  • You're supposed to stay to the right except when passing.
  • You are supposed to scare visitors. You are not supposed to actually bite anyone. You don't have to memorize many lines.
  • We aren't supposed to fly.

16Should have и could have: прошлые сожаления

Modal + have + V3 для прошлого.

  • Should have / shouldn't have + V3 — разочарование, сожаление, критика: «надо было, стоило».
  • Could have + V3 — возможность в прошлом, которой не воспользовались: «мог бы».

Примеры

  • You should've talked more about your hard skills. I should've prepared for the interview.
  • I could've prepared better. You could've lied about croissants.
  • Could've answered — могла бы ответить. Should've answered — надо было бы ответить.
  • Could've started from this. Why did I have to explain…?
  • The show shouldn't have been canceled.

17Must have и might have: предположения о прошлом

Modal + have + V3 для предположений. На доске эта тема была только в примерах.

Примеры

  • You've probably seen it. You must have seen it. You might have seen it.

18Comparatives и superlatives

  • Comparatives — для сравнения существительных и наречий, в том числе с глаголами чувств (seems quieter).
  • (not) as + adjective / adverb + as — такой же / не такой, как.
  • Quantifiers a bit, a little, slightly, much, a lot — с прилагательными и наречиями: slightly more expensive.
  • Superlatives — для сравнения с группой: the least expensive, the furthest. С by far, much — для усиления.
  • Emphasising: повтор сравнительной степени (more and more dangerous, faster and faster), than ever (better than ever), ever с превосходной степенью.
  • Parallel comparatives: the + comparative, the + comparative.

Примеры

  • The more you practise, the better you become.
  • The bigger the project, the more responsibilities I have.
  • The higher you are, the more responsibilities you have.
  • The more I work, the more I understand.
  • We've had more forest fires this year than ever before.

19Clauses of purpose

Справочник clauses of purpose:

  • to + infinitive, in order to + infinitive — чтобы назвать цель действия.
  • so as to + infinitive — то же, но более формально. Подлежащее главного предложения и придаточного цели одно и то же.
  • so that / in order that + subject + verb — когда подлежащие одинаковые или разные. Если подлежащие разные, используют только so that или in order that, НЕ in order to, to или so as to.
  • Отрицание: not ставят перед to — in order not to, so as not to. Просто not to возможно, но обычно только для контраста. После so that и in order that — обычное отрицание.
  • Порядок: придаточное цели обычно идёт после главного, но может стоять первым — тогда после него ставят запятую.

Примеры

  • I'm going downstairs to lock up. I often turn off my phone in order to avoid distractions.
  • The police put up barriers so as to stop people entering the building.
  • I'd like to move closer to the stage so that I can hear better.
  • The school is changing the exam date in order that students have more time to prepare.
  • He set his alarm for 7 a.m. in order not to oversleep. I check my 'to do' list every day so as not to forget anything.
  • I eat a lot of vegetables, not to lose weight but to improve my health.
  • Let's check this very carefully so that we don't make a mistake.
  • The company gave us our money back in order that we wouldn't post a negative review.
  • In order to buy a car, I had to save up for a year.
  • I am learning English to / in order to get into a university.
  • She decided to have a baby so that her child can teach her some memes. Natasha is learning memes so her colleagues laugh at her jokes.

20Verb patterns: gerund и infinitive

Справочник 1B verb patterns: иногда два глагола идут вместе (They hope to learn Spanish. I like playing ball games). Второй глагол стоит в форме -ing:

  • после предлога (кроме to), если первый глагол требует предлога: think about doing, succeed in finding, believe in telling, concentrate on passing, dream about playing, apologise for getting, struggle with working, care about saving;
  • после phrasal verb: carry on driving, look forward to reading, give up studying, put off making, end up carrying;
  • после некоторых глаголов: stop buying, keep telling, can't resist doing, like / hate doing, spend time doing, start doing.

У try и stop значение меняется:

  • try + -ing — сделать что-то как эксперимент, чтобы проверить, поможет ли; предложить решение.
  • try + to — приложить усилие. В прошедшем времени обычно о неудачной попытке.
  • stop + to infinitive — прервать одно действие, чтобы сделать другое.
  • stop + -ing — закончить или бросить действие совсем.

Примеры

  • You should think about doing that course. I finally succeeded in finding a job.
  • She apologised for getting angry. He struggles with working at night.
  • We carried on driving for another hour. I look forward to reading your new book! We put off making that decision.
  • If you can't sleep, try drinking some warm milk. Have you tried restarting your computer?
  • I tried to open the door, but it was locked.
  • We stopped to have lunch. He stopped smoking last year. Please stop talking.
  • She couldn't stop buying new things because she loved following fashion trends.
  • Have you tried …ing? I've stopped …ing. I can't resist …ing. I don't feel like …ing.

21Причина и следствие: due to, lead to, cause

Due to + noun — из-за чего-то.

Due to the fact that + clause — из-за того, что.

It can lead to, it can cause — для следствия.

Despite the fact that — для уступки.

Примеры

  • Due to that situation…
  • Due to the fact that you don't do homework, you are not progressing.
  • Why are we so hooked on social media? Due to the fact that it's enjoyable.
  • Watching can lead to… It encourages lack of activity. It can cause psychological problems.

22Would rather и would prefer

Конструкции для выбора и предпочтений.

Примеры

  • Would you rather have tea or coffee?
  • I'd definitely prefer to look at the options before deciding.
  • Would you prefer to be rich or famous?
  • I'd rather send her a message than phone her.

23There + be

There + be + noun — о существовании чего-то.

It + be + adjective — описание.

Примеры

  • There should be… There must be…
  • There will be a storm. There will be an earthquake.
  • It will be rainy.

24Avoiding repetition: so, not, be (7D)

Справочник 7D: чтобы не повторять сказанное, используют другое слово вместо глагола или фразы.

  • Вспомогательные глаголы do, does, did заменяют глагол в Present или Past Simple.
  • so и not: think so, hope so, tell someone so — для положительного ответа. Также suppose so и guess so, когда соглашаемся, но не очень хотим. Для отрицания — hope not, suppose not, guess not. С think чаще говорят don't think so.
  • to: want to, would like to, try to заменяют глагол.
  • be: с be говорят want to be, would like to be, try to be.
  • Отрицания: don't want to, try not to, don't want to be, try not to be. Wouldn't like to (be) встречается реже.

Примеры

  • I think you know John? — Yes, I do. Susan thinks Ben doesn't like jazz, but he does. I didn't see John, but Mary did.
